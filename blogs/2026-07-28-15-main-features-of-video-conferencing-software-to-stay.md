[navigation] Group meetings If you’re going to build a solution that allows for one-to-one video calls only, you condemn it to failure from the start. A modern video calling app is a universal solution for gathering groups of people for a variety of purposes, such as parties, workouts, lessons, and business meetings. No wonder that apps like Cisco Webex, Skype, Zoom, Google Meet, and FreeConference allow for 100 participants and thousands of visitors .
